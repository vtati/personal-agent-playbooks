# 01 — Scope (LinkedIn Pipeline)

## IN — this kit rebuilds

- The LinkedIn unit as a side-chat pipeline: charter, per-post approval
  contract, reporting cadence (`06-operating-model.md`).
- The profile overhaul: the headline/About/experience/settings template
  in `playbooks/linkedin-overhaul-playbook.md` — fill it in during
  setup so a fresh profile or a re-check starts from your approved
  copy, not from memory.
- **AI Dev Digest**: daily research sweep → 5-story slate (PRODUCT /
  FRAMEWORKS / REPO / RESEARCH / ECOSYSTEM) → POSTER-skin 8-slide
  carousel → per-edition owner approval → live publish + same-session
  first comment.
- **Concept posts**: week plan → evening finalize (fresh research, full
  copy, carousel/infographic built + visually QA'd) → owner's
  `schedule`/`edit:`/`skip` → native scheduler → first comment.
- **AI Week Digest**: Sunday best-of-the-week edition.
- Supporting jobs: weekly calendar, first comments (weekday + Sunday),
  engagement monitor, topic options.
- The three carousel builders (`scripts/`) and the digest Front Page
  structure + skin system (`08-brand-and-standards.md`).

## OUT — deliberately not in this kit

- **Credentials.** The LinkedIn password lives in the owner's vault only
  (named in doc 05). The agent never holds it in chat or files.
- **[YOUR EXCLUDED PROJECT] content.** OUT of this lane by the owner's
  decision — name one project or topic that is deliberately excluded
  from this lane (in the original lane it was a product the owner moved
  to a different lane mid-flight); no topics, plugs, or ship posts
  about it here.
- **Employer material.** Employer work is positioning copy only (public,
  quantified, already on the profile). No employer code, data, or
  confidential detail ever enters a post.
- **Cohort internals.** If your sprint is tied to a course or cohort,
  the provider and instructors are credited by name (yours, from the
  worksheet) but course content is never revealed — curiosity, not
  content.
- **Live engagement history / the post queue.** Not in this kit — they
  are live state on your account; LinkedIn itself is the source of
  truth.

## Reference vs fresh files

- Reference (carry over as-is): all 12 docs, `jobs/`, `scripts/`,
  `playbooks/`, `brand/`.
- Fresh on the new account: chat IDs, device ID, browser profile,
  vault entries, `hidden_files/linkedin-queue.json`,
  `hidden_files/engagement-log.jsonl`, `digest-edition.txt` (seed at 1
  on a fresh setup, per the worksheet).
