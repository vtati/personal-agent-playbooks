# 08 — Brand & Standards (LinkedIn Pipeline)

## Persona

First-person builder. Crisp, concrete, numbers-first. Zero fluff
adjectives ("passionate", "synergy" are banned). The profile and posts
read like someone who would rather show a shipped system than describe
a vision. Hashtags: 3–5, Title Case, end of post. One genuine question
per post — never engagement bait ("comment YES" is penalized).

## Positioning (approved copy lives in the playbook)

- Headline: `[YOUR HEADLINE]`
- Framing: "[YOUR BRAND] is an AI lab exploring AI applications
  across industries — building, showcasing, and problem-solving."
- Legal entity in any formal context: **[YOUR COMPANY LLC]** — never
  the old company name.
- Profile mechanics: Follow is the primary button; custom URL
  [YOUR LINKEDIN URL]; pinned skills: [YOUR 3 PINNED SKILLS —
  a field skill, a specialty skill, a discipline skill].

## Digest standards (locked)

- Front Page structure + POSTER skin per doc 03A. Avatar on slides:
  `brand/avatar.jpg` ONLY (never any other photo file).
- Post copy formula: `AI Dev Digest #<N> — every morning, 5 stories for
  builders. Here's today's:` + 5 one-line teases (no URLs) + a genuine
  technical question + `Follow for tomorrow's edition. Same time, same
  place.` + 3–4 Title Case hashtags (always #BuildInPublic).
- First comment: 5 labeled primary-source links + `Deeper video
  breakdowns: YouTube @[YOUR NEWS CHANNEL] — https://www.youtube.com/@[YOUR NEWS CHANNEL]`.

## Concept-post standards

- 150–250 words; first line must earn the "see more" click; links only
  in the first comment; handles as plain text in the body.
- Carousel bullets ≤ ~125 chars (longer tails truncate silently — check
  every slide's last bullet visually).
- Visual QA is mandatory: render every slide to PNG and look at it —
  cover contents collisions and recap overflows have both shipped
  before being caught; they are caught in QA now, not after posting.
- Palette (owner-steered 2026-10-19): lean strong orange (#FF7A00) +
  blue + ink black for sketchnotes; avoid pink-dominant palettes. Icons:
  drawn SVG in solid chips — never pale emoji as functional icons (they
  render near-invisible).

## First-comment channel block (concept posts)

Include only what's relevant to the post:
- YouTube @[YOUR YOUTUBE HANDLE] "builds & deep-dives" — https://www.youtube.com/@[YOUR YOUTUBE HANDLE]
- YouTube @[YOUR NEWS CHANNEL] "daily AI news" — https://www.youtube.com/@[YOUR NEWS CHANNEL]
- Instagram @[YOUR INSTAGRAM] · @[your-news-channel]
- Portfolio [YOUR PORTFOLIO URL]

## Standing content rules

- [YOUR EXCLUDED PROJECT]: OUT of this lane — no topics, no plugs. (Name your own excluded project/topic in the worksheet at setup.)
- Every digest story verified against a primary source; never invent
  progress, numbers, or versions.
- Posts plug your second channel where relevant (if you run one):
  its details + a follow ask in the first comment.
