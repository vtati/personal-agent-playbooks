## The concept in one line
Workslop = forwarding the effort, not the work. The sender saved 20 minutes; the receiver lost two hours.

## The angle we can own (the open lane)
A 10-second smell test the reader can run on any polished AI-looking document:
1. Is there a decision in it — or just a summary of everything?
2. Is there a number someone stands behind?
3. Is there a named owner for the next step?
No decision, no number, no owner → it's a costume for work, however clean it looks.

## Two turns to pick between in brainstorm
- Turn A (reader-side): "How to spot workslop in 10 seconds" — the smell test above; ends asking readers for the most polished-but-empty thing they've received.

The agent's lean: Turn A for reach (everyone has received it), with Turn B's checklist folded in as the closing paragraph.
