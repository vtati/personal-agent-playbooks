## How the daily job uses this
1. Web/social search for each voice's recent (24–48h) AI posts/takes (X, blogs,
   newsletters usually mirror LinkedIn).
2. Periodically (weekly), a logged-in browser scan of LinkedIn itself refreshes this
   list and notes recurring themes.
3. Substance over follower count — hype without technical content stays out, even
   from big names.
