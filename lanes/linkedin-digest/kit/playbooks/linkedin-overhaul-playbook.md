## 1. The two scores, two jobs

**A. Profile Strength — "All-Star"** (LinkedIn's internal completion meter, visible only to you under "Suggested for you" on your profile)
Levels: Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced → Expert → **All-Star**. To hit All-Star you need: profile photo, location, industry, headline, position, education, skills, summary/About, 50+ connections. Once complete, the meter disappears.
*What it does:* All-Star profiles rank higher in LinkedIn search results and get more search appearances. It's a **completion** metric, not a quality metric — a complete but bland profile still gets All-Star.
Sources: [LinkedIn Help — profile level meter](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a594698) · [2026 optimization checklist](https://www.linklulu.com/blog/linkedin-profile-optimization)

**B. Social Selling Index (SSI)** — 0–100, free at linkedin.com/sales/ssi. Four components, 25 pts each:
1. Establish your professional brand (profile completeness + posting + engagement on your content)
2. Find the right people (search/networking activity)
3. Engage with insights (sharing + commenting)
4. Build relationships (connecting with decision-makers, response rate)
Target **70+** for top tier. This is the closest thing to a "recruiter score" for your personal brand, and the posting schedule in §5 is what moves it.

**What LinkedIn Premium actually adds** (so you buy it with eyes open): gold Premium badge, AI-powered profile-writing suggestions, InMail credits, "who viewed your profile" depth, applicant insights on jobs. Premium does *not* change the All-Star or SSI formulas — those are free. Buy it for the AI suggestions + recruiter InMail surface, not for a score boost.

## 2. Section-by-section audit & fixes

### Banner
Make one banner image (exact 1584×396, PNG, <8MB) that restates your positioning in one line: **[YOUR ROLE] — [YOUR ONE-LINE PROOF STRIP: employer/program · venture · years]**. Text sits in the center safe zone — clear of the profile-photo overlap (bottom-left on desktop) and edge cropping on mobile. Best practice: one clear message, not a stock photo ([Taplio checklist](https://taplio.com/blog/linkedin-profile-optimization-tips)). Upload: profile → pencil icon on banner → upload PNG → save.

### Profile photo
Use your best headshot: face centered, fills the 400×400 circle crop. No logo, no distance shot. (This same photo, cropped square, becomes `brand/avatar.jpg` for the digest slides.)

### Headline — rewrite (220 chars max; only ~60–70 show in search/comments, so front-load)
**Fill-in formula:**
> [YOUR ROLE KEYWORD — the exact title recruiters search] | [YOUR PROOF POINT — employer/program/transformation] · [YOUR SPECIALTY] | [YOUR VENTURE LINE, e.g. "Founder @ [YOUR BRAND]"]
Why: opens with the exact search keyword, then proof points before the fold. Never just repeat the job title with no keyword and no result.

### About — rewrite (sweet spot 300–500 words; first line is the hook before "see more")
**Fill-in skeleton (the original followed exactly this shape):**
> [HOOK: one line — what you turn ideas into, and where: inside enterprises, inside your own ventures, or both.]
>
> [CURRENT ROLE PARAGRAPH: your role, your employer/program if public, and 2–3 quantified wins — a platform you built, a process you sped up (with the %), a manual task you automated (with before → after times). Numbers or it didn't happen.]
>
> [BUILDER PARAGRAPH: your side venture/lab in one or two lines — what it explores, 2–3 example projects by name.]
>
> [WHAT YOU WRITE ABOUT + follow ask, one line.]
>
> Open to: [YOUR TARGET ROLES], and [advisory/consulting conversations if wanted].
> 📩 [YOUR EMAIL] · 🌐 [YOUR PORTFOLIO URL]

### Experience — mirror your portfolio/resume (recruiters cross-check; consistency = trust)
1. **[CURRENT ROLE]**, [EMPLOYER] · [START]–Present · [LOCATION]. 3–4 quantified bullets (platform/systems you own; % improvements; time saved; scope).
2. **[YOUR VENTURE ROLE, e.g. Founder]**, [YOUR COMPANY/BRAND] · [START]–Present. One line on what the venture is + its 2–3 flagship projects.
3. **[PREVIOUS ROLE]**, [EMPLOYER] · [DATES]. Strategy/scope bullets with numbers (programs covered, technologies evaluated).

### Featured — pin proof, not promises
Portfolio site, your best product page/demo, a certification-prep or resource page if you have one, and 1–2 flagship posts once the content engine runs.

### Skills — pin the top 3 recruiters search
Pin: *[YOUR SKILL 1 — usually your field, e.g. Artificial Intelligence], [YOUR SKILL 2 — your specialty], [YOUR SKILL 3 — your architecture/discipline]*. List 8–12 total incl. your stack (platforms, frameworks, methods). Then get 5+ endorsements on the pinned three from colleagues.

### Recommendations — the most underused lever
Request 3: one peer/leader from your current role (delivery story), one founder/venture collaborator (building story), and one collaborator from your lab/brand. Give each a one-line prompt ("could you speak to the [PROJECT] work?") — specific asks get written.

### Settings most people miss
- **Custom URL:** claim linkedin.com/in/[your-name] (ditch the numeric suffix).
- **Make Follow primary:** Settings → Visibility → Followers — visitors see Follow; Connect sits in More. (The digest engine depends on this conversion setup.)
- **Open to Work** (recruiters-only visibility): titles *[YOUR TARGET ROLES]*; locations [YOUR LOCATIONS / remote].
- **Contact info:** portfolio URL + email visible.
- **Industry:** [YOUR INDUSTRY]. **Location:** [YOUR METRO AREA].
- **Connections:** 500+ is the credibility threshold — connect with every conference contact, contributor, and peer; accept inbound daily for 2 weeks.

### How top profiles are structured (the pattern)
Hook headline (role keyword + proof) → banner that restates positioning → About as a pitch (value prop → 2–3 proof points → CTA), not a chronology → experience with quantified bullets → featured proof → pinned searchable skills → recommendations. Every section answers "why should a recruiter bet their fee on this person" in the first line.

## 5. Day-to-day posting schedule (fully automatable)

**Cadence rationale:** LinkedIn's own guidance: 2–5 posts/week is the sweet spot; members posting 2×/week see ~5× more profile views; top-10% B2B creators average ~4.1×/week ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/top-linkedin-feed-questions-2025-gyanda-sachdeva-anl2e), [Ghost 2026](https://www.growwithghost.io/blog/linkedin-posting-frequency-for-founders-how-often-should-you-post-in-2026/)). Daily posting is the aggressive end — it works *if* every post clears the quality bar; one filler post costs more than a skipped day. The calendar below is 7 days with an explicit "skip rather than ship filler" rule. Post at **[YOUR SLOT — the original used 8:30 AM ET]** (peak window 8–10 AM, Tue–Thu strongest).

### The 7-day rotation (3 pillars)
| Day | Pillar | Post | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | 🔔 AI updates | "AI Radar" — one significant release/update + your builder's take: what changes for people shipping AI | Native text, 150–200 words |
| Tue | 🛠️ How-to | Mini-tutorial from your real stack (your platforms, patterns, tools) | Carousel/doc or numbered text post |
| Wed | 🔔 AI updates | Midweek signal — model eval result, pricing change, policy shift + implication | Native text |
| Thu | 📚 Learn AI | One curated resource (paper, repo, course) — what it teaches, who it's for, the 1 key insight | Native text + link **in first comment** |
| Fri | 🛠️ How-to | Weekend-build tutorial — small project readers can ship in a day | Carousel/doc |
| Sat | 🔔 AI updates | Week-in-AI recap: 3–5 stories, one-line take each | Native text |
| Sun | 📚 Learn AI | Learning path / founder lesson — "if I were learning agents from zero, week 1 would be…" | Native text |

**Format rules (algorithm, Sept 2026):** publish natively, full text in the post — never a link-only teaser; external links go in the first comment ([SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/linkedin-algorithm)); prefer native documents/carousels over external blogs; 3–5 hashtags; no engagement bait ("comment YES" is penalized) — ask one genuine question instead; stay on 2–3 topics for 90 days so the recommender learns your lane ([360Brew analysis](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/understanding-linkedins-360brew-algorithm-krista-mollion-7cc3e)).

### The automation pipeline (what "fully automated" really means)
Honest constraint: LinkedIn's API does **not** allow auto-publishing to personal profiles — any tool claiming one-click personal auto-post violates ToS and risks the account. What *is* fully automatable:
1. **Weekly draft engine (scheduled job, Sundays 6 PM):** sweep AI news/releases → map 7 topics to the pillar calendar → draft all 7 posts in your voice (first-person builder, concrete, 150–250 words) → save to a review doc.
2. **You approve in ~10 min** (edit/skip any).
3. **Queue via LinkedIn's native post scheduler** (built into the composer — schedule each for your morning slot).
4. **Daily 10-min engagement block:** reply to every comment within 60–90 min of publish — the algorithm's strongest quality signal.
5. **Monthly review:** top 3 posts by saves/comments → double down on those topics.

The rest of this kit (docs 03, 07, 10 and `jobs/`) is exactly this pipeline, productionized.
