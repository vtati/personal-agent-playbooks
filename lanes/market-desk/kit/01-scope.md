# 01 — Scope

IN:

- Daily research-desk coverage of your accounts at [YOUR BROKER]:
  pre-market brief, intraday trigger watch (3 checkpoints), close wrap,
  weekly idea + playbook-refinement proposals (inside the Friday wrap),
  monthly regime refresh + call scorecard.
- Holdings analysis (fundamentals, analyst ratings, technicals, earnings
  calendar) and written adjustment recommendations for the main/other
  accounts. YOU execute those trades manually.
- Autonomous order placement on a dedicated pilot/agent account only —
  if your broker offers one — under your standing autonomy grant, with
  human trade approvals ON (you tap approve in your broker's app), every
  action logged in TRADE-LOG.md and reported the same day. If your broker
  has no agent account, this lane is read + recommend only, and that is a
  complete, valid configuration.
- Playbook stewardship: the playbook is a living document; changes are
  proposed in Friday wraps / monthly reviews and applied by the
  interactive advisory flow, never silently by a scheduled run.

OUT:

- Trading the main or other accounts (out of scope by charter; with an
  MCP-style connection it is also typically impossible — reads only).
- Options, margin, shorts, leveraged ETFs (pilot rules, Playbook R16).
- Licensed financial advice: the desk gives research calls with reasons;
  you decide on your accounts.
- Other lanes/projects — separate setups with their own kits; do not
  conflate.
- Tax filing or legal advice; tax-loss harvesting is executed as
  portfolio hygiene and flagged for your tax adviser where the rules are
  gray (doc 09).
